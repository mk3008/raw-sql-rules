# Repository work

Use `raw-sql-rules.md` as the normative source and `RATIONALE.md` and
`EVIDENCE.md` for product reasoning and evidence limits.

Astra performs repository work directly; do not use a routine orchestrator or
delegate implementation, checks, or interpretation. When an explicitly needed
fresh-agent test cannot be answered deterministically, use Luna with medium
effort and the task's stated budget. Do not substitute another model.

Research snapshots, frozen packets, and recorded example instructions describe
their original experiments. Preserve them as historical evidence; they do not
set the workflow for new repository work.
